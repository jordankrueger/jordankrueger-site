Verdict

Re-skin jordankrueger.com in the Solarpunk look you picked: cream, leaf green and sun yellow, Bricolage Grotesque type, and a sun, solar panel and circuit-vine drawn around your photo. The words and page layout stay. Because the site already keeps its colors and fonts in one settings file, most of the change is swapping those values; the new parts are the logo, the hero drawing, a circuit-line divider and the blog "vine". Everything is built on the private dev site (jordans-mac-mini:4322). Nothing is pushed to GitHub until your headshots are in and you say "ship it", because pushing creates a public preview link.

Steps — reuse before adds

Step 1 — colors and fonts

Reuses the existing color names in global.css and the self-hosted font setupAdds new values, Bricolage files, a reduced-motion setting

Re-skins most of the site at once. Then about 20 hard-coded colors in 11 files get pointed at the shared colors.

Step 2 — header and footer

Reuses the existing header, including its phone menu buttonAdds the sun-and-leaf logo and the Subscribe pill

Step 3 — homepage

Reuses Hero, newsletter, blog cards, contact formAdds the hero drawing, the circuit divider, the blog vine

Step 4 — other pages

Reuses every existing card componentAdds polish where needed, and a small Star Trek console strip on About

Step 5 — checks

Design checklist (the "humanizer" for design), screenshots of every page on desktop and phone, a color-contrast test that is proven able to fail, a keyboard pass, and a clean build.

Step 6 — headshots and launch

Your photos go in at the placeholder names. Only after you say "ship it": pick up any posts published meanwhile, push, open the pull request, merge, then check the live site, including real test submissions of both forms to your own address.

Decisions

Decided

Nothing is pushed until launch.

A pushed branch gets a public preview link showing the placeholder headshots.

Decided

Keep the existing color names; only change their values.

The Tools page's content files refer to four of them by name, so renaming would mean editing content for no visual gain.

Decided

One font family, hosted on the site, loaded the same way as today.

Astro now has a newer fonts feature; switching to it in the same change adds a second thing that can go wrong. It can be a later, separate change.

Deferred

Dark mode.

The site has none today, and it would double the contrast and drawing work.

Open question

  1. Remove the emoji from four headings? (“Hiya & welcome! 👋”, “About me 🤔”, “🔧 What I Do”, “🧠 What I'm Known For”)

    Removing them makes the headings match the drawn style and drops a common AI-design tell. Keeping them keeps the old playful tone. The emoji list in the About headline (Star Trek, synthwave, technology) stays either way.

    Recommended: remove them from headings, keep emoji in body text. Easy to undo later: one line per heading.
