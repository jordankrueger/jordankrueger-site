Verdict

The redesign is split into six slices that run in order. Slices 1–4 are each built by a Sonnet worker in its own copy of the dev branch, then reviewed by me before it's merged into the dev site. Slice 5 is my whole-site check, and slice 6 is your headshots plus launch. The live site stays as it is until slice 6, and only after you say "ship it". The new palette was checked before writing this: every text-and-background pair passes the accessibility contrast standard (lowest is 4.67 to 1, against a 4.5 minimum). Full plan: docs/superpowers/plans/2026-10-25-solarpunk-redesign.md; design: the spec companion.

The six slices — reuse before adds

Slice 1 — colors, font, hard-coded colors

Reuses the existing color names, the self-hosted font setupAdds new color values, Bricolage Grotesque (one file), a contrast test, reduced-motion setting

A small test checks every text color against its background. It is written first and fails on today's colors, then passes after the swap, and is proven able to fail again on purpose. About 20 hard-coded colors in 11 files get pointed at the shared colors. Worker: Sonnet, own copy of the branch, own preview port.

Slice 2 — header and footer

Reuses the existing menu, phone menu button and linksAdds the sun-and-leaf logo, the Subscribe pill, a one-band footer

The handwritten Rock Salt logo is retired. Every footer link is kept; a check compares the old and new link lists so nothing gets dropped.

Slice 3 — homepage

Reuses Hero, newsletter, blog cards, contact formAdds the drawing around your photo, the circuit divider, practice cards, the blog vine

The "Hiya & welcome!" and "About me" headings lose their emoji. The spam-check widgets and form settings on the newsletter and contact forms are explicitly off-limits; only their look changes.

Slice 4 — remaining pages

Reuses every existing card componentAdds per-page polish, and the small Star Trek console strip on About

About, AI, Projects, Tools, the blog list, blog posts, and the small pages. One commit per page.

Slice 5 — whole-site check

Reuses the design checklist and screenshot reviewAdds nothing new

Done by me in the main session: the design "humanizer" checklist on every page, the screenshot critique loop, a keyboard pass, a Codex review of the full change, then the dev link goes to you.

Slice 6 — headshots and launch

Reuses the placeholder file names already wired inAdds your photos

Only after you say "ship it": pick up any new posts, push, pull request, wait for every check, merge, watch Cloudflare until the new version is live, then check the live site, including real test sign-ups to your own address.

Rules every slice follows

Rule

No pushes and no pull request until slice 6.

A pushed branch gets a public preview link with placeholder photos on it.

Rule

No content changes except the four heading emoji.

You asked to keep the content; only the look changes.

Rule

Form spam checks and form settings are off-limits.

Restyling must not break sign-ups; slice 6 proves it with real test submissions.
