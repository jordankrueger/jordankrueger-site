20 requests to paste into Claude

Swap in your own details where you see brackets. If Claude ever changes something you didn't want, just say "undo that."

Make it look like you

  1. "Interview me with five questions about who I am and what I do. Then write my About page from my answers."
  2. "Change the site name to [your name] and the tagline to [a short line about you]."
  3. "Show me two color and font options that feel [warm / professional / playful] before you change anything."
  4. "Here's my photo. Put it on the homepage and About page, and make sure it looks good on a phone."
  5. "Add links to my social accounts in the footer, with small icons: [list your accounts]."

Add pages

  1. "Add a Contact page with my email and links to my social accounts."
  2. "Add a Projects page with a card for each of these, including a short description and a link: [list]."
  3. "Add a Services page listing what I offer, with a 'Get in touch' button on each."
  4. "Add a photo gallery page using these images."
  5. "Add a Resume page from this text: [paste your resume]."
  6. "Add a 'Now' page that says what I'm focused on this month. Keep it short and easy for me to update."

Write posts

  1. "Write a new blog post from these notes, in my voice: [paste notes]."
  2. "Turn this email or social media post into a blog post: [paste it]."
  3. "Make the homepage show my three newest posts with a short summary of each."

Grow your audience

  1. "Add an email signup form to the bottom of every blog post."
  2. "Make sure every page has a clear title and description, so it looks good in Google and when someone shares the link."
  3. "Add free, privacy-friendly visitor stats using Cloudflare Web Analytics, and tell me where to see them."
  4. "Help me get my site listed in Google. Walk me through it step by step."

Keep it working

  1. "Check the site on a phone-sized screen and fix anything that looks cramped."
  2. "Something looks wrong on my [page name] page. Here's a screenshot. Find and fix it, then tell me in plain English what you changed."
