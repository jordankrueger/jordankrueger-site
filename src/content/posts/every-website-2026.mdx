Daniel Miessler recently [published a list](https://danielmiessler.com/blog/sites-we-launched-2026) of every site he and his AI assistant launched in the last 18 months. There are 40 of them. I read it and realized I'd never counted mine.

It's 22. Some are brand new; some are old Carrd pages I finally rebuilt. AI did most of the typing on every one of them (mostly Claude, some Codex), and I think that's the real story.

A few years ago, each of these would have been a decision. Is this idea worth a weekend, or a developer, or a few thousand dollars? Most of them would have stayed ideas. Now a site costs about as much as the idea does, so the only question left is whether the idea is any good. (Some of these may not be! That's fine too.)

Here's the list, grouped by topic.
